Build an interactive 3D model of the human heart that runs in my browser and teaches how one heartbeat works.

What it is for:
I'm showing it live to an audience. Most of them are not doctors. They should understand a heartbeat after two minutes with it, and a medical student should find nothing wrong in it.

What I should be able to do:
- See a beating heart in 3D, rotate and zoom it, and cut it open to see the four chambers and the four valves working.
- Watch blood move through it in the right order: body, right side, lungs, left side, body. Colour it by oxygen level, and say in a note that the blue is a drawing convention.
- Watch the electrical signal travel: SA node, atria, AV node (with its delay), bundle of His, Purkinje fibres, ventricles.
- See a live ECG trace drawn in time with the beat, so the P wave, the QRS complex and the T wave line up with what the heart is doing at that moment. Add a pressure and volume chart for the left ventricle beside it.
- Hear the two heart sounds, made in code, each one exactly when its valves close.
- Pause, go one phase at a time through the cardiac cycle, and slow it down to a tenth of the speed.
- Change the heart rate from 40 to 180 beats a minute and see what really changes: the resting phase shortens far more than the pumping phase.
- Click any part to get its name and what it does in two plain sentences.
- Switch to three conditions and see how the motion, the flow and the ECG change: atrial fibrillation, complete heart block, and aortic stenosis.
- Switch between Arabic and English. Arabic lays out right to left, and each medical term keeps its English name beside the Arabic.

Rules:
- Accuracy comes first. The timing of the phases, the order the valves open and close, the pressures and the ECG must match standard physiology. If you simplify something, list it in a "What this model simplifies" panel. If you are not sure of a number, leave it out.
- Build the heart from your own geometry in code. No models, pictures or code taken from other sites.
- A line at the bottom says this is for learning, not for diagnosis.
Do not come back to me until everything is done.

How to work:
- Give me your plan in a few lines, then build without waiting for me.
- When it's built, open it yourself and check it against the cardiac cycle: at each phase, are the right valves open, is the ECG at the right wave, do the sounds land on the valve closures? Check all three conditions the same way. Fix what's wrong.
If you hit a session limit, make a checkpoint and continue when it's restarted.
If you hit a usage limit, make a checkpoint until I switch accounts.
